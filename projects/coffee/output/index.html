Instagram endorsement graph: an arrow A → B means A tagged or @mentioned B in a recent post; a dashed line means Instagram suggests one as related to the other. Circle size = followers.
Food blogger IG related
Links between affiliated accounts (sister shops, owners tagging their own shop) are excluded. Data: public Instagram profiles (logged-out view exposes each account's latest 12 posts). "Bloggers" / "Makers" = distinct bloggers / makers who tagged the account. "Related" = accounts in the graph whose Instagram related suggestions include it; related links are not counted as endorsements. Creator "Taste" = PageRank over the endorsement graph (a tag from a well-endorsed account counts more). (normalized so 1.0 = average). Blogger "Taste" = for each maker they tag, log₂(1 + number of other accounts that also tagged it), summed — high when they spot makers others independently vouch for.
